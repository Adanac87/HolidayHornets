Holiday Hornets

The holidays are a time to meet new people, spend time with friends and do fun things together.

Michael Sorgiovanni started Holiday Hornets when he lived in Vancouver, and now he's bringing it back. It's a way to see what's happening around the city each season, meet new people and do things together, whether you join the group, bring a friend or make it a date night. The important thing is that you get out there and enjoy the city.

Browse the list, tap Interested on anything you'd join, and we'll set dates for the outings with the biggest swarm.
